Delete Your Account

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your HomeSchooling app account and the data associated with it, published by Pawel (developer of the HomeSchooling app on Google Play).

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to paweliitian@gmail.com from the email address associated with your HomeSchooling account.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my HomeSchooling account", and include the email address you sign in with.
  3. We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days, and will confirm by email once it's done.

Removing a single child's data without deleting your account

If you only want to remove one child's profile rather than your whole account, you can do this yourself directly in the app: open that child's profile and choose "Remove child." This deletes that child's profile and associated activity data immediately, without affecting your account or any other children on it.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (name, email address, password).
  • All child profiles linked to your account (names, level, interests, goals).
  • All learning activity, progress and skill records linked to those children.
  • Guardian verification and consent records linked to your account.

What may be retained, and for how long

We may retain a minimal record of the deletion request itself (such as the fact and date that an account was deleted) for up to 12 months, for fraud-prevention and legal/audit purposes. We may also retain data for a limited period where required by applicable law, after which it is deleted. No learning content or personal profile data is retained beyond the deletion itself.

Questions

Contact paweliitian@gmail.com with any questions about this process. See also our Privacy Policy.
