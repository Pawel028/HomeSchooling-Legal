Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the HomeSchooling app ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when a parent or guardian ("you") and the children they manage use the App.

1. Who this App is for

The App is intended to be used by parents or legal guardians to plan and track homeschooling activities for their children. Parents/guardians create and control the account; children's activity within the App happens under a parent/guardian's supervision and account.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: the parent/guardian's name, email address and/or phone number, used to create and secure the account and to sign in (including one-time passcodes sent to verify identity).
  • Child profile information: a child's name and grade/age band, entered by the parent/guardian, used solely to personalise learning plans and activities.
  • Learning activity and progress data: which activities are assigned, started, or completed, and related progress/skill records, used to plan curriculum and show progress to the parent/guardian.
  • Consent records: where the App asks a parent/guardian to consent to a child's participation in specific activities or data processing, we record that consent.

We do not knowingly collect information directly from children without a parent or guardian first creating and controlling the account.

3. How we use this information

  • To operate the App's core features: creating homeschool plans, assigning activities, and tracking progress.
  • To authenticate accounts and keep them secure (including one-time passcodes for sign-in).
  • To communicate with the parent/guardian about their account or the App.
  • To improve the App's curriculum content and reliability.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising. The App does not display ads.

4. Where information is stored

Data is stored on secured cloud infrastructure (Microsoft Azure, hosted in India) with access-controlled databases. Access to any individual family's data is restricted, at the database level, to that family's own account.

5. Sharing of information

We do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers (such as our cloud hosting provider) strictly to operate the App, under confidentiality obligations, and may be disclosed if required by law.

6. Data retention

Account and child profile data is retained for as long as the account remains active, and for a reasonable period afterward in case the account is reactivated, unless a deletion request is made (see below) or deletion is required sooner by law.

7. Parental rights and controls

As the parent/guardian, you can:

  • Review and update your account and your children's profile information within the App.
  • Withdraw consent for a specific activity or data use.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated children's data at any time by contacting us (below). We will act on such requests within a reasonable time, except where we are required to retain certain records by law.

8. Children's privacy

The App is designed to be used by children only under the direct account and supervision of a parent or legal guardian, who provides any information about the child and controls the account. We do not knowingly allow children to create their own independent accounts or to provide personal information without a parent/guardian's involvement.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect information, including encrypted network connections, database-level access controls that restrict each family's data to that family, and access restrictions on our backend systems. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.
